Compound

Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 September 2026

Compound is a private fitness and body-composition tracker built and run by one person for their own use. It has no advertising, no analytics, no tracking pixels, and no third-party SDKs. Your data is not a product here, and there is no business model that depends on it.

This policy explains exactly what Compound stores, where it lives, and the one case where information leaves infrastructure under the operator's control.

Who runs Compound

Compound is operated by an individual, not a company. Contact: griffin@gobblegobble.com.

What Compound stores

Only what you enter or explicitly connect. There is no background collection, no device fingerprinting, and no location tracking.

  • Body data — body weight, body-fat percentage, waist and lean-mass figures, and progress photos you choose to take.
  • Food — calories and macronutrients you log.
  • Training — workouts, exercises, sets, reps, weight, reps-in-reserve, cardio sessions, heart rate and calories you enter, session notes and how a session felt.
  • Sleep and daily notes — sleep duration, notes, and subjective ratings.
  • Medication — if you use the GLP-1 feature, the compound, doses and injection sites you record.
  • Settings — your profile figures (age, sex, height, activity level), goals, and preferences.

WHOOP data

If you connect a WHOOP account, Compound reads — and only reads — the following. It never writes anything to WHOOP and never posts on your behalf. It does not request access to your WHOOP profile, so it never receives your name or email address from WHOOP.

ScopeWhat it is used for
read:recoveryDaily recovery score, heart-rate variability and resting heart rate. The same response also carries blood-oxygen percentage and skin temperature, which are stored but not currently displayed.
read:cyclesDay strain, and the daily figures recovery is filed against.

A rule the app enforces

WHOOP data never overwrites a value you typed. WHOOP's figures are kept in a separate store, so they cannot displace anything you entered. Sleep is the single field WHOOP will fill, and only when you have left it blank and have not marked that day as skipped. Once you edit a figure, it is never touched again.

Where your data is kept

  • On your device. The app stores your data in your browser's local storage. It stays on the device until you clear it.
  • On private hardware. If you enable syncing, your data is copied to a personal server (PocketBase) on the operator's own hardware, reachable only over a private Tailscale network. It is not exposed to the public internet and is not hosted with a cloud provider.
  • WHOOP access tokens. Held on a private server, in a file readable only by the service account, and never sent to your browser. The WHOOP client secret is likewise only ever on the server.

Who your data is shared with

It is not sold, rented, traded, or shared for advertising — ever. There is one disclosure worth being precise about, and one hosting fact.

The optional coach check-in

Compound includes an optional written check-in feature. When you ask for one, a snapshot of your training and nutrition data — body weights, food logs, sleep duration, training sessions and exercises, and GLP-1 entries if you use that feature — is sent to Anthropic's API to generate the written summary. Anthropic states that data submitted through its API is not used to train its models.

Your WHOOP recovery score, heart-rate variability, resting heart rate and strain are not included in that snapshot. Sleep duration is included, and WHOOP may have been what filled it. Progress photos are never included. If you never use the check-in feature, nothing is ever sent.

Hosting

The app itself is a static page served by GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub receives ordinary request information such as your IP address and browser user-agent when the page loads. Your logged data is never sent to GitHub — it is not part of the page.

Beyond those two, no third party receives your data. There is no analytics provider, no crash reporter, no advertising network, and no data broker.

How long it is kept

Your data is kept for as long as you keep using the app, because the whole point is a long-term record of your training. Nothing is deleted on a schedule, and nothing is archived elsewhere. When you delete it, it is gone.

Your choices and how to delete things

  • Disconnect WHOOP. Settings → WHOOP → Unlink. The stored access and refresh tokens are deleted from the server immediately and no further data is requested. WHOOP figures already pulled into the app remain in your own records until you delete them.
  • Revoke from WHOOP's side. You can also revoke Compound's access in your WHOOP account settings at any time, independently of this app.
  • Export everything. Settings → Data & Backup exports your data as a file you keep.
  • Delete everything. Settings → About & Reset erases the data on the device, and clearing the app's site data in your browser removes anything left.
  • Ask. Email griffin@gobblegobble.com to have server-side copies deleted.

Security

Traffic between the app and its servers uses HTTPS. The sync server and the WHOOP service sit on a private Tailscale network rather than the open internet. Credentials are stored server-side with restricted file permissions. No system is perfectly secure, and this is a personal project rather than an audited commercial service — that is stated plainly rather than glossed over.

Children

Compound is not intended for anyone under 16, and is not directed at children.

Health information

Compound is a personal logging tool. It is not a medical device, it does not provide medical advice, and nothing in it should be used to diagnose or treat anything. Talk to a doctor about medical decisions, including any medication you track here.
